Cài đặt

IA v2, tab 4. Cài đặt (paper header, no wordmark) keeps the profile card, Mục tiêu mỗi ngày, Ứng dụng, the Pro card and Tài khoản. Hồ sơ is a pushed screen with the same fields as today's Hồ sơ (sex, birth year, weight, goal, default training minutes), no new fields. /settings/targets is read-only: the computed targets with their formula lines, and one button to Hồ sơ. Sample profile: Nam, sinh 2003, 65 kg, Giữ cân. Copy is Vietnamese; these notes are English.
