Nhật ký

IA v2, tab 2. Nhật ký owns the day (any date): the only place with a day's full breakdown. Title row with the date nav; the ink day summary (kcal dial, four macro bars, "còn N kcal"); two rows for the day, Vi chất (/micros?date=) and Ngày tập (/training?date=); meals with one + and one ⋯ each. Past days are read-only: no +, no ⋯, rows open read-only screens. Sample data: lib/mock/sample-day.ts (today, Thứ Hai 12/10, 1.041 kcal) and lib/mock/yesterday.ts (past sample, Thứ Sáu 09/10, 1.532 kcal). Copy is Vietnamese; these notes are English.
