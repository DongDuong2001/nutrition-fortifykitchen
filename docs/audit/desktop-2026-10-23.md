# Desktop audit, 23/10/2026

What works on the phone but is missing, awkward or broken with a mouse and keyboard at 1280, 1440 and 1920 wide.
Measured on main 8eeb7b7 (demo build, seeded day) in Chromium, with a WebKit pass at 1280. Two walks:
A covered Hôm nay, Thêm món, the food page, Đủ 22 vi chất, a nutrient page, Ngày tập, Tổng quan and Chia sẻ; B covered Khám phá
and its sub-pages, Cài đặt, Hồ sơ, Có gì mới, the tour, onboarding and sign-in. 54 raw findings (30 + 24), merged into the 29 rows
below; the checks that passed are summed up at the end.

Severity: **must** = a task is hidden, unreachable or broken by mouse or keyboard; **should** = works, but clearly worse
than on the phone or wastes the screen; **nice** = polish.
Status: **fixed** = in fix/desktop-parity (small, inside each component's existing `lg:` classes, phone unchanged);
**proposal** = a layout change, for Hao and the reviewer to mock up first (no new layouts in the fix PR).

## What already works

- Every dialog (the + dialog, Khay · xem lại, So sánh, Chia sẻ, date picker, ⋯ menu, template and saved-meal dialogs, tips, the tour) is centred (420 / 480 / 900 px) or an anchored 340 px popover, takes focus inside, traps Tab (0 of 15 Tabs left the + dialog), closes on Esc (nested ones one at a time) and returns focus to its opener in Chromium.
- The + dialog shows "Enter để thêm · Esc để đóng"; Tab order is close › meal › search › chips › list.
- Focus rings on every Tab stop (3 px cobalt); Tab order follows the visual order.
- Dragging a food between meals works with the mouse; press-and-hold 500 ms starts select mode.
- The mouse wheel scrolls the content on every screen, the food page's micro list included (job 10 could not reproduce a stuck column on main).
- The 240 px sidebar fits at 1280×800; no desktop click target is under 32 px (the training checkboxes sit in 44 px labels); no desktop text under 12 px except the /micros legend.
- Tổng quan's four sub-tabs are links with the right `aria-current`.
