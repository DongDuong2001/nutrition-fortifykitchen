### Decisions
- Today is in progress: it is neither met nor missed in the dots and in "n/7 ngày đạt", and its bar is grey; the day line
  says "đang ghi". This matches the averages, which never count today.
- Above the target range counts as on target (the product rule already in `lib/period.ts`), so the day line reads
  "đạt mục tiêu" and never "vượt mức".
- 30-day view: value, change and Vi chất are for the 30 days; the dots, "n/7" and the bars are the last 7 days of the
  period, as the spec says.
- Vi chất squares are a 6-column grid (8 at ≥1024) so each square is a 44 px target; the mock used 11 columns of 22 px.
- Hero label is set in Saira Condensed (uppercase, 13 px) so it passes the "no all-caps outside Saira" probe in cc2_unify.
- Hero "Phụ" bar is Trước tập + Bữa phụ together.
