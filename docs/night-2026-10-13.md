## cc2-21 — Tổng quan sub-tabs Năng lượng, Vi chất, Cơ thể in the Tóm tắt system

### What changed

  `NutrientGrid`, `AttentionRow`. Tóm tắt now uses them too (`TodayHero`, `Scoreboard`, `MicroCard`, `AttentionList`).
- `lib/overview-detail.ts` (new, tests first): `macroScoreRow`, `weightScoreRow`, `trainingSplit`, `bestWorstDays`,
  `energyAttention`, `microSections`, `topFoodSources`, `nutrientAttention`, `weighIns`, `weightShift`.
  `lib/overview-board.ts`: `underDaysRow`, `BoardKey` (carbs, fat, fibre rows).
- Năng lượng: hero = average kcal vs the range; rows Kcal, Đạm, Chất béo, Tinh bột (g and % of kcal), Chất xơ*;
  kcal split bar; training vs rest days; closest and farthest day; Cần chú ý (kcal, đạm, chất xơ days under).
- Vi chất: hero n/22 with the 22 squares (name, share, level, link to /micros/[nutrient]); sections Thiếu, Trung bình,
  Gần đủ, Đạt (native `<details>`, plus "Chưa có số liệu" when a nutrient has no number); Cần chú ý = the 3 lowest with
  amount / target, the gap per day and the logged foods that gave the most.
- Cơ thể: hero = latest weight and the change in the period; the weight line (a point or the ‹ › stepper selects a
  weigh-in); weigh-ins n/7, change vs the previous period, since the first weigh-in; kcal, đạm and kali targets
  before → now.
- Tests/probes: `tests/probes/cc2_21.py` (390 and 1280); `cc2_10.py` updated (energy bars read the Kcal row's bars,
  the average comes from `#en-hero-number`, Vi chất rows are counted inside `#mi-sections`); `alignment.py` groups
  for /trends (`overview-rows`), /trends/micros (`overview-micro`), /trends/body (`overview-stats`).

### Decisions
- Training vs rest days read the duration saved per day by Ngày tập (`lib/training-checklist`). A day with no saved
  duration is unknown and counts on neither side; with none saved the card says so and links to Ngày tập.
- "Closest" and "farthest" day: distance outside the kcal range (0 inside). No farthest day when all days are inside.
- Foods that gave the most use the finished logged days of the period (today is not counted, like the averages).
- The weight line uses a full-height button per weigh-in; on 30 days a column is narrower than 44 px, so the stepper
  (44 px buttons) is the accessible path.
- Targets before → now use the first and the latest weigh-in of the period with the profile's sex, goal and
  training minutes (`calculateTargets`); nothing is invented.

### Open risks
- Sections for Vi chất show nutrients with no number under "Chưa có số liệu" instead of 0.
- Detail links drop the period query (`/micros/[nutrient]`); the nutrient screens do not read it.
