## The problem

The first serving is the one the app shows as "per portion". Matcha and Hạt Brazil list 100 g first (Matcha has no other serving), and Matcha Satoen lists 100 g before its spoon, so "per portion" shows the amounts of 100 g of a powder or a nut. One teaspoon of matcha is 2 g, one Brazil nut is 5 g.

- Labels are the ones the live rows already use (`1 muỗng cà phê`, `1 hạt`, `1 nắm`, `100 g`). The only gram weight that is new on a row is Matcha's 2 g, which is Hao's figure and the same weight Satoen already has live; 5 g and 28 g are live.

## What an update changes

The importer upserts the whole row: it rewrites `name`, `name_vi`, `category`, `brand`, `tip_vi` and the nutrients and replaces the food's portions. The API does not return `name` or `brand`, so they are copied from the import records the live rows came from (`Matcha (powdered green tea)`, `Matcha (powdered green tea) [Matcha Satoen]` with brand `Satoen`, `Nuts, brazilnuts, dried, unblanched`); `name_vi`, category, `tip_vi` (none) and nutrients were compared with the live rows: identical. A diary entry already logged stores its own snapshot (`portion_label`, `grams`, `nutrients_snapshot`), so past entries keep their numbers; only new entries use the new first serving.

`docs/data/servings-2026-10-23-before.json` is the same three rows with today's live servings: importing it puts them back (rollback).
