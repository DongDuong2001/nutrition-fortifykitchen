## Read this first (3 things that differ from the brief)

1. **There is no USDA "Molasses, blackstrap" record to take the missing minerals from.** I checked the SR Legacy release (April 2018, the final one) in `food-research/usda/sr_legacy`: its only molasses record is FDC 168820 "Molasses" (the regular one, which the brief rules out); Foundation 2026-04-30 and FNDDS 2024-10-31 have no blackstrap entry either, and a FoodData Central search for "blackstrap molasses" (SR Legacy, 23/10/2026) returns 0 results. So the two blackstrap rows (Golden Barrel and Brer Rabbit Blackstrap) are **label only**: magnesium, copper, manganese, zinc, phosphorus, selenium, B6 and the others the label does not list are left out, not copied from FDC 168820. If Hao wants them filled, a real source is needed (a published blackstrap analysis, or a lab sheet from the maker); I did not look beyond the brief's source order.
2. **The labels give the serving in mL, not in grams.** Brer Rabbit, Grandma's and Golden Barrel all print "1 tbsp (15 mL)". See "The one assumption" below: the per-100 g values divide by **20 g per tablespoon, the weight USDA SR Legacy gives for molasses (FDC 168820, portion "serving 1 tbsp" = 20 g)** and the weight these rows already use in the live catalogue.
3. **The "Mật mía Rabbit" row keeps `usda_sr_legacy` / `FDC 168820:mat-mia-rabbit-song`, as the brief says, but its values are now label values.** The app will show a USDA source tag on a Brer Rabbit label row. Changing it means a new `source_id` (a new row), so I left it for Hao.

## The one assumption: 20 g per tablespoon

Every nutrient is exact per 15 mL (the label's serving); only the step to per 100 g needs a weight. I used USDA's 20 g per tablespoon of molasses (SR Legacy FDC 168820, `food_portion.csv` row "serving 1 tbsp", 2 data points). The same record gives 337 g per cup, which is 21.1 g per US tablespoon (14.8 mL) or about 21.4 g per 15 mL; a 15 mL spoon of molasses really weighs about 21 g. With 21.4 g every per-100 g value below would be about 7 % lower (kcal 280 instead of 300, close to USDA's 290 for the generic record). I kept 20 g because it is the USDA figure for exactly this unit and the rows already use it; the 1 muỗng canh = 20 g serving shows 60 kcal, the label's own number, so the spoon in the app matches the jar. If Hao prefers the density figure, the five serving weights change to 21, and all per-100 g values are ×(20/21.4).

## Row decisions

- **Which Brer Rabbit variant is the old "Mật mía Rabbit" (6598c0aa…)?** Its values are USDA FDC 168820 (regular molasses, per 100 g: 290 kcal, Ca 205, Fe 4.72, Mg 242, K 1464, Na 37), its name in the import notes was "proxy for Rabbit brand sugarcane molasses", and its source_id names no variant. Against the three labels (per 100 g at 20 g) the minerals do not decide: Full Flavor and Mild are about equally far (summed |log ratio| over Na, Ca, Mg, Fe, K: Full 2.71, Mild 2.61) and Blackstrap is far (4.94, Ca 950 and Fe 24 against 205 and 4.7). I took **Full Flavor** (Brer Rabbit's standard grade, as USDA's generic "Molasses" is the regular grade) for the update and added Mild and Blackstrap as new rows. It only decides which row keeps the old id; if Hao wants Mild, swap the two records' `source_id`s.
- **Brand** is now "Brer Rabbit" on that row (it had none); brand and name are rewritten by the importer's upsert, so all five records carry brand, `name` and `tip_vi` (none, as before).
- **Servings** of every row: `1 muỗng canh` = 20 g first, `100 g` second (the live rows listed 100 g first, which shows 100 g as the portion). The brief's 1 muỗng canh = the label's serving grams; the label serving is 15 mL, see above.
- **Golden Barrel keeps `source` and `source_id` exactly** (`label` / `Golden Barrel Blackstrap Molasses (label panel image not retrievable)`), so `lib/training-foods.ts` still finds the Ngày tập carb food: it looks the row up by that pair first, then by name. The source_id text says the panel was not retrievable; it now is (see below), but the string is the lookup key and stays.
- **%DV to mg, Golden Barrel only** (the other four labels print mg): its panel is the **new FDA label** (it has an "Added Sugars" line, 22 % DV, and lists vitamin D, calcium, iron, potassium), so I used the new daily values: Ca 1300, Fe 18, K 4700 (as in the brief) and, for the minerals the brief does not list, Mg 420, Zn 11, Mn 2.3, Cu 0.9 mg. Sodium is printed in mg (30 mg). Each %DV is a whole percent, so these mg are ±3 % for Ca, Fe, K, Mg, Mn, Cu and **±50 % for zinc** (1 % could be 0.5 to 1.5 %); vitamin D "0 %" is left out. Cross-check: Brer Rabbit Blackstrap's printed 190 mg Ca and 370 mg K are 15 % and 8 % of the same DVs.
- **Added sugars** (`added_sugars`) is kept where the label prints it (Golden Barrel 11 g, Grandma's 15 g per serving); Brer Rabbit's page prints only total sugars, so it has none.
- **Emoji 🍯:** `lib/food-emoji.ts` has the rule `["mật ong", "mật mía"] → 🍯`. I replayed the rule list from the file on the five `name_vi` strings (and the old "Mật mía Rabbit"): all five match that rule first, none is caught by an earlier rule. Nothing to change in the web code.
