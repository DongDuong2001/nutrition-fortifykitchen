# Data Sources for H1T MICRO Nutrition Catalog

1. **Vietnam Food Composition Table (Viện Dinh dưỡng Quốc gia)**
   - Coverage: Traditional Vietnamese dishes, common market foods, street foods.
   - Version: 2007 / 2016 updates.
   - Usage: Vietnamese local dishes and recipes.

2. **USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy + Foundation Foods)**
   - Coverage: Foundation raw ingredients, comprehensive micronutrient profiles.
   - Licence: CC0 Public Domain.
   - Link: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/

3. **Canadian Nutrient File (CNF)**
   - Coverage: Canadian national food composition reference.
   - Licence: Open Government Licence – Canada (Attribution).
   - Link: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-nutrition/healthy-eating/nutrient-data.html
